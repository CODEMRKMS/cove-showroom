  Three interview questions

  1. Why store a hash of the refresh token, and why rotate it?
     A database leak then doesn't hand out usable sessions. Rotation makes each
     token single-use, so a stolen token shows up as reuse, and we respond by
     revoking the user's sessions.

     A JWT stays valid until it expires. A demoted or deleted user would keep
     their old privileges for up to 15 minutes if we trusted the claim. One
